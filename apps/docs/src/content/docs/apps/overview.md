With **Flow-Like Apps**, you can manage and organize your automations within dedicated projects.

While **Flow-Like** refers to the overall platform—the "operating system" you access, for example, by opening the Flow-Like desktop app—**Flow-Like Apps** are the specific projects you create and work in.

Each **Flow-Like App** enables you to:

- Build and organize [Flows](/apps/boards/) in the visual [Studio](/studio/overview/),
- Build custom [chat interfaces](/apps/chat-ui/) and connect them to your **Flows**,
- Create rich [custom UIs](/apps/a2ui/) with drag-and-drop or AI generation,
- Link **Flows** to the outside world through [Events](/apps/events/),
- Configure [secrets and API keys](/apps/runtime-variables/) securely per device,
- [Upload files and folders](/apps/storage/),
- Model, explore, and share data in [Data Studio](/apps/data-studio/),
- [Share and collaborate](/apps/share/) with your team,
- Snapshot and pin [Flow versions](/studio/versioning/) for stable entry points,
- Reuse your ideas and automations with [Templates](/apps/templates/).

![Anatomy of a Flow-Like app, connecting its Flows, experiences, data, reusable building blocks, and delivery controls](../../../assets/FlowLikeAppAnatomy.svg)

An App is the project boundary. Its Flows contain the executable logic, while
Events and Pages expose that logic as experiences. Storage, Data Studio,
Widgets, Templates, roles, and release metadata stay alongside the Flows that
use them.
