This required 20-minute capstone includes one 15-minute recipe lab and a 5-minute review. Open the recipe you selected in the introduction from the course contents and finish its practical steps. If you already completed it, use that result for the review. The other four recipes remain optional.

Record:

- The chosen recipe, its fixture and the actual trigger/runtime.
- The expected effect and where you inspected it.
- A repeated-run result and one failure or unavailable-dependency result.
- What remains before a real deployment, including any concurrency or credential setup your lab did not implement.

Use the recipe's fixture expectations to judge the output. A log-only prototype is evidence of its tested steps, not proof that an unconnected send or write works. The quiz below checks decisions across recipes and does not automatically inspect your app.
