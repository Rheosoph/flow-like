Review a change using the access evidence, without repeating the versioning procedure.

Take a harmless update to the practice Flow, such as changing its displayed result. Prepare this record before moving an Event to the new version:

| Field | Evidence to include |
| --- | --- |
| Proposed change | User-visible behavior before and after |
| Scope | Affected Flow, Event, data and permissions |
| Candidate | Exact numbered version and test result |
| Approval | Reviewer and decision time |
| Recovery | Previous tested version and restoration check |

Changing a default role or exposing a route needs renewed access testing even if the edit is one line. A compatible output correction may have a smaller scope. Classify by the effect on callers and data.

Have another person review the record, or perform a clearly labelled practice review yourself. Promote only within the synthetic app and rerun the affected allowed and denied operations. Keep credentials out of the record.

For a publication request that is on hold, address feedback on the existing request. The app supports one active pending/on-hold request. Approval changes visibility; the reviewed version and access configuration still need their own verification.

Completion: the record identifies what changed, who reviewed it and the observed result. Use the next lesson to locate the corresponding product audit evidence.
