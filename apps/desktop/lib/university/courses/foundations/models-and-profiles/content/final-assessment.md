Priya joins the support team on Monday. Your job before then: hand over the two-gear setup and make sure it survives her first week. Everything you need was covered in the five lessons — this page only gives you the artifacts.

## The requirements

- **R1 — Train drafting.** Replies get drafted on the commuter train. Customer text must not leave the laptop, connection or no connection.
- **R2 — Production drafting.** In the office, drafts run on a hosted model with room for very long ticket threads — and customers love attaching screenshots.
- **R3 — Policy search.** The flow finds the closest policy passage by meaning, not by keywords.
- **R4 — Voicemails.** Customer voicemails arrive as audio and must become text.

## The setup you're handing over

- Two profiles: a lean local gear and a stocked production gear, switched from the upper-left menu.
- One drafting flow that selects its model through preferences instead of naming one.
- A policy index built last quarter with the embedding model still assigned today.
