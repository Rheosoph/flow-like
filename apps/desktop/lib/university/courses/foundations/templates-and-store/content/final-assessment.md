Quarter-end. The support kit is real, the rebuild era is over — and this week decides whether it stays that way. Everything below is the state of the world; the challenges ask you to make the calls.

## The kit

Three flows, all living in the **Customer Support Copilot** app (online, currently **Prototype**, 4 team members):

- **Intake Triage** — proven, versioned at 2.1.0, the flow every client project starts from.
- **Reply Drafter** — working, but the draft currently carries half-finished experiments nobody has reviewed.
- **Weekly Digest** — stable at 1.3.0.

## The template shelf

- An **Intake Triage** template was created in June, source set to **Latest** at the time.
- In July, the triage draft received a critical routing fix and was versioned to 2.1.0.
- The June template has exactly one version.

## The asks

1. **Spin-up speed** — any teammate must stand up a new client app in under an hour, using the team's own blueprints.
2. **The neighboring team** wants the triage graph for their own project — nothing else. Not your storage, not your events, not your roles.
3. **The partner org** wants to evaluate the kit by forking it. Compliance's one hard rule: client rows must never leave the app. Structure may.
4. **Friday, 10:00** — a live demo where the partner opens the store listing and requests access. Today is Monday.

## Dry-run findings

A new teammate ran a rehearsal on a fresh laptop and logged four findings:

- **F1** — She installed the team's mail package from Explore → Packages, opened the client app's flow, and found none of its nodes in the catalog.
- **F2** — After she applied a package update in Library → Packages, an older client app that had been running fine started failing.
- **F3** — During an earlier store experiment, a visitor briefly saw last night's half-finished Reply Drafter behavior.
- **F4** — A client app created from the June template still shows the June routing bug — the July fix never arrived.
