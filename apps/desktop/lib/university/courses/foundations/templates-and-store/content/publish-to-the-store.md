Publication is a separate decision from reusing a template. Inspect an online practice App's publication controls and prepare its name, purpose, intended users, and release notes. Do not submit merely to finish this class.

Public and Public Request follow the store publication process; review requirements and timing depend on the deployment. Check the current status and instructions in the publication interface rather than promising a fixed approval date.

Before submission, identify the tested Flow versions that live Events should use and review access and fork contents. The App's display version is metadata; it does not snapshot the graph or pin an Event.

**Check:** your publication draft lists the tested behavior and exact version targets, plus any outstanding review or access work. Follow **Release and Rollback** for release pinning and **App Governance** for public access decisions.
