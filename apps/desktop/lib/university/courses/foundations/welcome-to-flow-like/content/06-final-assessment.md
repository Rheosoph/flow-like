## The prototype

On your laptop lives an **offline App** called *Research Digest*. Its single Flow does one job: an event starts the run, a node reads files from a folder on your laptop, a drafting step summarizes their contents, and a final node writes a summary record. You built it for yourself, signed out, in an evening.

## The ask

A teammate watches it run and wants in: "Put it in the browser for the whole team. And schedule it nightly — it should run whether your laptop is open or not." She also has opinions about the handover, delivered over coffee:

- "Switch to my Profile on your machine — then the app is mine, right?"
- "Just flip the app online in its settings."
- "If the write step keeps misbehaving, drag it further right so it runs later."

You'll be asked what actually works.

## The incident

Last night's test run reports a green check: **1.9 s**, no errors. This morning the summary record contains *yesterday's* text. Nothing crashed; something is still wrong.
