Hayro 0.7.1, hayro-interpret 0.7.0, hayro-syntax 0.7.2, hayro-jbig2 0.3.0, hayro-cmap 0.1.0
and hayro-postscript 0.1.0 (https://github.com/LaurenzV/hayro), MIT OR Apache-2.0, retain their
upstream licence files. The first five are patched sources transferred from PdfCraft
(https://github.com/storytold/pdfcraft): hayro-cmap from revision
a2ec27b4775f53c40cad3456936d9406611e3a8d, the others from revision
ec0a965074f376a5bf610435948e4e5184bf3299. hayro-postscript is the crates.io 0.1.0 package
(upstream revision a2354aebaf86f5ab13e5fd6b6b0521306488bf9d) with one local patch.
See vendor/README.craft-reader.md and each crate's SOURCE.json for provenance and local changes.

from PdfCraft pull request #2770 by Guillem Hermida, commit
6fcf2a4aca29e943e28cb69017ff8f87d00656c6 (https://github.com/storytold/pdfcraft/pull/2770),
MIT OR Apache-2.0; crates/pdf/tests/plates.rs and tests/support/synthetic_icc.rs adapt its tests.

#2974 by mtlcl410, commit bae69819594a6385a027fec1f67e6b3c36c259b4
(https://github.com/storytold/pdfcraft/pull/2974), MIT OR Apache-2.0;
crates/pdf/tests/knockout.rs adapts its test.
The reader retains the existing Foxit/PDFium standard-14 font assets (BSD-3-Clause),
Compact ICC Profiles by Clinton Ingram (CC0-1.0), and dependency CMaps (BSD-3-Clause).
