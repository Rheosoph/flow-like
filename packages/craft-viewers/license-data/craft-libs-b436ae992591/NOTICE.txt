Optional DWG bridge

`craft-dwg` uses `acadrust` 0.6.6 unmodified as a separate dependency for DWG reading and writing.
It is licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. The license text and upstream third-party
notices are retained in `licenses/dwg/`; the crate is available from crates.io and
https://github.com/hakanaktt/acadrust.

The byte-level bridge source was moved from CADCraft `crates/dwg` at revision
`664837df8925f932e90a7ae37c58d2f54e7a1320`. The bridge itself is MIT OR Apache-2.0; its only
MPL-2.0 component is the unmodified `acadrust` dependency.
